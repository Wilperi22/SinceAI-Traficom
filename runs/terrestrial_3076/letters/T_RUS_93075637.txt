Subject: BR IFIC (Terrestrial Services) No. 3076 - land-mobile assignment 93075637

Dear colleagues,

The Administration of Finland has examined the new land-mobile assignment
93075637 (station class FB) published in BR IFIC (Terrestrial Services)
No. 3076. Our calculation indicates that the field strength produced on Finnish territory
exceeds the coordination trigger of CEPT Recommendation T/R 25-08 (Annex 1, A1.2.3).

Technical summary
  Assignment:                 93075637 (adm. ref. 0330FB2026), 152.9 MHz, 16.8 kHz, F3E--
  Transmitter site:           KRL LYASKELYA, 61.7606 N 30.9894 E
  e.r.p. / antenna height:    17.4 dBW / 26 m
  Worst point in Finland:     land border, 62.0108 N 30.3751 E (42.5 km)
  Path:                       Land 42.5 km
  Model:                      ITU-R P.1546-6 (Py1546), 10% time, 50% locations, h2=10 m, Rural, no terrain
  Calculated field strength:  20.9 dB(uV/m)
  T/R 25-08 trigger:          20.0 dB(uV/m)
  Exceedance:                 0.9 dB
  Finnish stations affected:  none in the special-interest list above the trigger

In view of the above, the Administration of Finland kindly requests that you
  1. confirm the technical characteristics of the assignment, and
  2. coordinate the assignment with the Administration of Finland before bringing it into use,
     or reduce the field strength at the border below the T/R 25-08 trigger.

The Administration of Finland reserves its rights with respect to protection of its
radiocommunication services from harmful interference.

Yours faithfully,
Traficom - Spectrum and Space
