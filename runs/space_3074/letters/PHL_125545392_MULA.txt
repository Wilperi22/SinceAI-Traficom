Subject: BR IFIC 3074 - satellite network MULA, notice 125545392:
         power flux-density over Finnish territory - exceeding RR Table 21-4

Dear colleagues,

The Administration of Finland has examined the API/A information for the satellite
network MULA published in BR IFIC (Space Services) No. 3074. Our analysis
indicates that the notified characteristics may produce a power flux-density (pfd) at the
Earth's surface over Finnish territory that exceeds the limits of Article 21, Table 21-4 of the
Radio Regulations.

Technical summary
  Notice / network:           125545392 / MULA
  Orbit:                      non-geostationary orbit, altitude 600 km
  Assignment:                 2222.7289-2223.2711 MHz, beam STX, emission 51K5G1D--
  Service (criterion row):    METSAT/SR/SO/EESS (2200_2300_SO_SR_EESS_METSAT)
  Regulation:                 RR Ed. 2024 Vol. 1 Art. 21 No. 21.16, Table 21-4 (Rev.WRC-23)
  e.i.r.p. in 4 kHz:          -12.3 dBW (emiss.pwr_ds_max x min(ref BW, emission BW) + peak gain)
  Worst-case geometry:        elevation 90.0°, slant range 600 km
  Calculated pfd:             -138.8 dB(W/m²) in 4 kHz
  Table 21-4 limit:           -144.0 dB(W/m²) in 4 kHz
  Exceedance:                 5.2 dB
  Finnish services (M4):      FIXED
  Finnish stations in band:   800000(RX), 800001(RX), 800002(RX), 800003(RX), 800004(RX), 800005(RX), 800060(RX), 800061(RX), 800062(RX), 800063(RX), 800064(RX), 800065(RX) (+52 more, see report)

Method: pfd = e.i.r.p. - 10·log10(4πd²), evaluated over Finnish territory at every reachable
angle of arrival; the limit is the angle-dependent value of the cited regulation. The calculation assumes
peak antenna gain toward Finland and is therefore a worst case.

In view of the above, the Administration of Finland kindly requests that you
  1. confirm the pfd values produced by MULA over the territory of Finland, and
  2. take the necessary measures (e.g. pfd masks, beam pointing restrictions) to ensure
     compliance with RR No. 21.16 and Table 21-4, so that Finnish services are protected.

The Administration of Finland reserves its rights with respect to protection of its
terrestrial and space services from harmful interference.

Yours faithfully,
Traficom - Spectrum and Space
