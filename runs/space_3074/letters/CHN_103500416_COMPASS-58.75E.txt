Subject: BR IFIC 3074 - satellite network COMPASS-58.75E, notice 103500416 (and related notice 303500416):
         power flux-density over Finnish territory - RR No. 9.14 coordination threshold exceeded

Dear colleagues,

The Administration of Finland has examined the Notification (11.2) information for the satellite
network COMPASS-58.75E published in BR IFIC (Space Services) No. 3074. Our analysis
indicates that the notified characteristics may produce a power flux-density (pfd) at the
Earth's surface over Finnish territory that exceeds the coordination threshold of RR Appendix 5, so that coordination with Finland is required
under No. 9.14 of the Radio Regulations.

Technical summary
  Notice / network:           103500416 / COMPASS-58.75E
  Orbit:                      geostationary orbit at 58.8°
  Assignment:                 2483.5-2500.0 MHz, beam SDN, emission 16M5G9W--
  Service (criterion row):    RDSS (AP5_2483.5_2500_RDSS)
  Regulation:                 RR Ed. 2024 Vol. 2 App. 5 Annex 1 Table 5-2, NOTES 9-10 (Finland) (No. 9.14)
  e.i.r.p. in 1 MHz:          42.2 dBW (emiss.pwr_ds_max x min(ref BW, emission BW) + peak gain)
  Worst-case geometry:        elevation 17.3°, slant range 39825 km
  Calculated pfd:             -120.8 dB(W/m²) in 1 MHz
  App. 5 threshold:           -128.0 dB(W/m²) in 1 MHz
  Exceedance:                 7.2 dB
  Finnish services (M4):      MOBILE; MOBILE-SATELLITE (SPACE-TO-EARTH); RADIODETERMINATION SATELLITE SERVICE (SPACE-TO-EARTH)
  Finnish stations in band:   none in the special-interest list

Method: pfd = e.i.r.p. - 10·log10(4πd²), evaluated over Finnish territory at every reachable
angle of arrival; the limit is the angle-dependent value of the cited regulation. The calculation assumes
peak antenna gain toward Finland and is therefore a worst case.

In view of the above, the Administration of Finland kindly requests that you
  1. confirm the pfd values produced by COMPASS-58.75E over the territory of Finland, and
  2. effect coordination with the Administration of Finland under RR No. 9.14 before the
     assignments are notified.

The Administration of Finland reserves its rights with respect to protection of its
terrestrial and space services from harmful interference.

Yours faithfully,
Traficom - Spectrum and Space
