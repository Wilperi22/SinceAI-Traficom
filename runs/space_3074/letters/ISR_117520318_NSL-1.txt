From: Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom, Spectrum and Space

Subject: BR IFIC 3074 - satellite network NSL-1, notice 117520318 (and related notice 317520318):
         power flux-density over Finnish territory - exceeding RR Table 21-4

Dear colleagues,

The Administration of Finland has examined the CR/C information for the satellite
network NSL-1 published in BR IFIC (Space Services) No. 3074. Our analysis
indicates that the notified characteristics may produce a power flux-density (pfd) at the
Earth's surface over Finnish territory that exceeds the limits of Article 21, Table 21-4 of the
Radio Regulations.

Technical summary
  Notice / network:           117520318 / NSL-1
  Orbit:                      non-geostationary orbit, altitude 520 km
  Assignment:                 18800.0-19040.0 MHz, beam DLDEM, emission 240MG7W--
  Service (criterion row):    FSS/ISS/METSAT (17700_19300_FSS_ISS_METSAT)
  Regulation:                 RR Ed. 2024 Vol. 1 Art. 21 No. 21.16, Table 21-4 (Rev.WRC-23), footnote 14, 15 (21.16.6A, 21.16.6B); fn 13 X = 0 for NGSO FSS
  e.i.r.p. in 1 MHz:          20.5 dBW (s_beam notified peak e.i.r.p. in ref. BW)
  Worst-case geometry:        elevation 90.0°, slant range 520 km
  Calculated pfd:             -104.8 dB(W/m²) in 1 MHz
  Table 21-4 limit:           -105.0 dB(W/m²) in 1 MHz
  Exceedance:                 0.2 dB
  Finnish services (M4):      FIXED; FIXED SATELLITE (SPACE-TO-EARTH)
  Finnish stations in band:   601452(RX), 602266(RX), 605729(RX), 623938(RX), 631021(RX), 634993(RX), 603413(TX), 628219(TX), 628728(TX), 632476(TX), 637357(TX), 647668(TX)

Method: pfd = e.i.r.p. - 10·log10(4πd²), evaluated over Finnish territory at every reachable
angle of arrival; the limit is the angle-dependent value of the cited regulation. The calculation assumes
peak antenna gain toward Finland and is therefore a worst case.

In view of the above, the Administration of Finland kindly requests that you
  1. confirm the pfd values produced by NSL-1 over the territory of Finland, and
  2. take the necessary measures (e.g. pfd masks, beam pointing restrictions) to ensure
     compliance with RR No. 21.16 and Table 21-4, so that Finnish services are protected.

The Administration of Finland reserves its rights with respect to protection of its
terrestrial and space services from harmful interference.

Yours faithfully,
Traficom - Spectrum and Space
