Subject: BR IFIC 3074 - satellite network LUMIRX-1, notice 124520203 (and related notice 324520203):
         power flux-density over Finnish territory - exceeding RR Table 21-4

Dear colleagues,

The Administration of Finland has examined the CR/C information for the satellite
network LUMIRX-1 published in BR IFIC (Space Services) No. 3074. Our analysis
indicates that the notified characteristics may produce a power flux-density (pfd) at the
Earth's surface over Finnish territory that exceeds the limits of Article 21, Table 21-4 of the
Radio Regulations.

Technical summary
  Notice / network:           124520203 / LUMIRX-1
  Orbit:                      non-geostationary orbit, altitude 517 km
  Assignment:                 9900.0-10400.0 MHz, beam SAR12R, emission ----SNSOR
  Service (criterion row):    EESS (9900_10400_EESS_active)
  Regulation:                 RR Ed. 2024 Vol. 1 Art. 21 No. 21.16, Table 21-4 (Rev.WRC-23), footnote 18 (21.16.8)
  e.i.r.p. in 1 MHz:          31.9 dBW (RR 21.16.8 mean pfd: pep_max 35.1 dBW x duty 0.015 (tau 15 us x PRF 1 kHz) over Bc 500 MHz + peak gain)
  Worst-case geometry:        elevation 5.5°, slant range 2079 km
  Calculated pfd:             -105.5 dB(W/m²) in 1 MHz
  Table 21-4 limit:           -113.0 dB(W/m²) in 1 MHz
  Exceedance:                 7.5 dB
  Finnish services (M4):      RADIOLOCATION; EARTH EXPLORATION-SATELLITE; Amateur; FIXED; MOBILE
  Finnish stations in band:   412266(RX)

Method: pfd = e.i.r.p. - 10·log10(4πd²), evaluated over Finnish territory at every reachable
angle of arrival; the limit is the angle-dependent value of the cited regulation. The calculation assumes
peak antenna gain toward Finland and is therefore a worst case.

In view of the above, the Administration of Finland kindly requests that you
  1. confirm the pfd values produced by LUMIRX-1 over the territory of Finland, and
  2. take the necessary measures (e.g. pfd masks, beam pointing restrictions) to ensure
     compliance with RR No. 21.16 and Table 21-4, so that Finnish services are protected.

The Administration of Finland reserves its rights with respect to protection of its
terrestrial and space services from harmful interference.
