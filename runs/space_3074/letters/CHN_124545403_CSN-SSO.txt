Subject: BR IFIC 3074 - satellite network CSN-SSO, notice 124545403:
         power flux-density over Finnish territory - exceeding RR Table 21-4

Dear colleagues,

The Administration of Finland has examined the API/A information for the satellite
network CSN-SSO published in BR IFIC (Space Services) No. 3074. Our analysis
indicates that the notified characteristics may produce a power flux-density (pfd) at the
Earth's surface over Finnish territory that exceeds the limits of Article 21, Table 21-4 of the
Radio Regulations.

Technical summary
  Notice / network:           124545403 / CSN-SSO
  Orbit:                      non-geostationary orbit, altitude 592 km
  Assignment:                 3600.0-3700.0 MHz, beam IMTSD6, emission 10M0G7W--
  Service (criterion row):    FSS (3400_4200_FSS_NGSO)
  Regulation:                 RR Ed. 2024 Vol. 1 Art. 21 No. 21.16, Table 21-4 (Rev.WRC-23), footnote 22, 23 (21.16.15, 21.16.16), Y = 0
  e.i.r.p. in 1 MHz:          43.2 dBW (emiss.pwr_ds_max x min(ref BW, emission BW) + peak gain)
  Worst-case geometry:        elevation 5.0°, slant range 2310 km
  Calculated pfd:             -95.1 dB(W/m²) in 1 MHz
  Table 21-4 limit:           -138.0 dB(W/m²) in 1 MHz
  Exceedance:                 42.9 dB
  Finnish services (M4):      MOBILE AND FIXED
  Finnish stations in band:   989011(RX), 985407(RX), 983816(RX), 983254(RX)

Method: pfd = e.i.r.p. - 10·log10(4πd²), evaluated over Finnish territory at every reachable
angle of arrival; the limit is the angle-dependent value of the cited regulation. The calculation assumes
peak antenna gain toward Finland and is therefore a worst case.

In view of the above, the Administration of Finland kindly requests that you
  1. confirm the pfd values produced by CSN-SSO over the territory of Finland, and
  2. take the necessary measures (e.g. pfd masks, beam pointing restrictions) to ensure
     compliance with RR No. 21.16 and Table 21-4, so that Finnish services are protected.

The Administration of Finland reserves its rights with respect to protection of its
terrestrial and space services from harmful interference.

Yours faithfully,
