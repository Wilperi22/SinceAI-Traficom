INTERFERENCE CHECK CRITERIA
===========================

Purpose
-------
Analyse the mandatory evaluation datasets separately:

  1. Space IFIC 3074
  2. Terrestrial IFIC 3076

The output must be technically reviewable. A frequency overlap is a candidate
for review, not automatically an interference error. A confirmed ERROR requires
an applicable technical protection criterion and a failed comparison.

The current ific3083.sqlite file must not be used as the mandatory 3074/3076
result unless its source issue and dataset type are verified. Keep the Space
and Terrestrial databases and result files separate.

Required input data
-------------------

A. Finnish national allocation data
   File: m4.json
   Source: https://opendata.traficom.fi/api/v13/Taajuusjakotaulukko
   Important fields:
     - Sub_band_lower_limit__Hz_
     - Sub_band_upper_limit__Hz_
     - Services_in_Finland
     - Sub_band_usage
     - Bandwidth
     - Comment
   Purpose:
     - Identify Finnish frequency allocations.
     - Identify the Finnish service and regulatory context.
     - Provide the frequency ranges for initial candidate filtering.
   M4 is not, by itself, a satellite PFD limit table.

B. Finnish special-interest station data
   File: Traficom materials/Traficom_SinceAI_Frequencies_of_interest_v05112026.xlsx
   Purpose:
     - Identify individual Finnish stations and protected uses.
     - Provide TX/RX centre frequencies and channel bandwidths.
     - Provide coordinates and station technical parameters.
     - Support terrestrial field-strength calculations.
   Important fields include station number, longitude, latitude, TX/RX
   frequency, channel bandwidth, antenna height, antenna gain, radiation power,
   radiation direction, directivity, and polarization.
   Transmitter power is not PFD and must not be compared directly to PFD.

C. Space IFIC 3074
   Source: ITU Space IFIC download service.
   Required notice families:
     - API/A
     - CR/C
     - AP30, including AP30A and AP30B
   Required output: separate Space IFIC 3074 candidate and error files.

D. Terrestrial IFIC 3076
   Source: ITU Terrestrial IFIC download service.
   Scope: foreign terrestrial land-mobile notices for the side quest.
   Required output: separate Terrestrial IFIC 3076 candidate and error files.

E. Regulatory and technical criteria
   Use the version applicable to the IFIC issue date and record the exact
   document, section, table, revision, and page in the audit output.
   Candidate sources:
     - ITU Radio Regulations: https://www.itu.int/pub/r-reg-rr
     - AP30/AP30A/AP30B provisions in the ITU Radio Regulations
     - ITU BR SpaceCom and GIBC tools/documentation:
       https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-R/software/Pages/space-network-software.aspx
     - ITU-R P.1546 for terrestrial field strength:
       https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-P.1546
     - CEPT/ECC implementation and applicable ECC decisions/recommendations:
       https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview
     - ECC T/R 25-08 where it is the applicable land-mobile criterion
     - Finnish Regulation M4 and, where relevant, M15:
       https://www.finlex.fi/fi/viranomaiset/maarayskokoelmat/traficom-viestinta/2026/3
       https://www.finlex.fi/fi/viranomaiset/maarayskokoelmat/traficom-viestinta/2025/51209

Before production analysis, create a criteria register containing:
  criterion_id, dataset, frequency range, service, direction, limit, unit,
  reference bandwidth, geographic condition, source document, source section,
  revision/date, and reviewer/status.

Common data-quality checks
--------------------------

Run these checks before technical comparisons:

1. Required fields are present and numeric where required.
2. Frequency lower limit is less than frequency upper limit.
3. Frequency units are normalized to Hz internally; report MHz for readability.
4. Bandwidth is positive and has a known unit.
5. PFD is finite and has a known unit and reference bandwidth.
6. PFD frequency interval overlaps its parent notice frequency interval.
7. Coordinates are valid WGS84 longitude/latitude values.
8. Antenna height, power, gain, and direction are either valid or explicitly
   marked unknown.
9. Notice IDs are unique within each source dataset.
10. Source issue, source file hash, import time, and conversion tool version are
    recorded.

A data-quality failure is not automatically harmful interference. Emit:
  REVIEW:DATA_QUALITY with the failing field and source row.

Frequency overlap rule
----------------------

Represent every frequency use as a half-open interval [lower, upper).
Two intervals overlap when:

  notice_lower < finnish_upper AND notice_upper > finnish_lower

A boundary touching at exactly one endpoint is not an overlap.

For each overlap, record:
  - notice ID and source dataset
  - notice frequency lower/upper limits
  - Finnish M4 band ID and limits, if matched
  - Finnish station number and interval, if matched
  - overlap lower/upper limits
  - emission bandwidth and receiver/channel bandwidth where available

SPACE IFIC 3074 CHECKS
=======================

S1. Notice-scope check
    Include API/A, CR/C, AP30A, and AP30B records. Record notice type and
    status/action. Withdrawn or superseded records must not be reported as new
    active errors, but may be retained for change history.

S2. Finnish frequency relevance
    Compare notice frequency intervals with M4 intervals and special-interest
    station intervals. Keep a candidate only when the frequency overlap rule
    succeeds. Record the Finnish service and station evidence.

S3. Service and allocation compatibility
    Check whether the ITU notice emission/service is compatible with or could
    affect the Finnish service in the overlapping band. The applicable M4,
    Radio Regulations, or AP30 provision must be cited. Frequency overlap alone
    produces REVIEW:FREQUENCY_OVERLAP, not ERROR.

S4. PFD source and unit check
    Prefer the notified PFD in the IFIC c_pfd table. Record:
      - pfd value
      - c_pfd frequency interval
      - c_pfd reference bandwidth (bdwdth)
      - PFD unit and sign convention
      - location/reference point and direction if supplied
    If the IFIC does not provide a usable PFD, use the authorized GIBC or
    SpaceCom calculation and record all input parameters and tool/version.
    Never compare PFD with Excel transmitter power.

S5. PFD normalization
    Normalize the notified PFD and regulatory limit to the same unit and
    reference bandwidth before comparison. Do not convert until the meaning of
    bdwdth and the PFD unit is confirmed. Record the conversion formula and
    source assumptions. An unknown reference bandwidth produces
    REVIEW:PFD_UNIT_OR_BANDWIDTH, not ERROR.

S6. PFD protection-limit check
    Select the limit from the applicable ITU Radio Regulations, AP30 provision,
    or an authorized GIBC/SpaceCom result. M4 and the special-interest Excel
    file identify Finnish use but do not automatically supply a satellite PFD
    limit.

    After normalization, an exceedance is:

      notified_pfd > applicable_pfd_limit

    Because PFD values are commonly negative dB values, -110 is greater than
    -118 and therefore exceeds a -118 limit.

S7. AP30-specific check
    For AP30A/AP30B notices, apply the relevant AP30 regulatory procedure and
    protection limits. Do not use a generic M4 threshold when an AP30-specific
    criterion applies. Record the AP30 section/table and all geographic or
    service-area conditions.

S8. Aggregate PFD check
    If the applicable criterion concerns aggregate interference, convert each
    contribution from dB to linear units, sum in linear units, then convert back
    to dB. Do not add dB values directly. Record all included notice IDs and
    exclusions.

S9. Space result classification
    ERROR only if all are true:
      - required notice type is in scope;
      - frequency overlap exists;
      - applicable service/geographic condition is satisfied;
      - PFD unit and reference bandwidth are compatible or normalized;
      - an authoritative limit is identified and cited; and
      - notified/calculated PFD exceeds that limit.

    REVIEW if there is overlap but the regulatory limit, unit, geometry, or PFD
    calculation is missing or uncertain.

TERRESTRIAL IFIC 3076 CHECKS
=============================

T1. Notice-scope and change check
    Use consecutive Terrestrial IFIC issues when available. Classify each
    foreign assignment as NEW, MODIFIED, WITHDRAWN, or UNCHANGED by comparing
    stable notice/assignment identifiers and relevant technical fields.

T2. Foreign-assignment filter
    Exclude Finnish administration records from the foreign-assignment side
    quest. Retain the administration code and country name in every result.

T3. Frequency and channel overlap
    Compare foreign assignment frequency/channel intervals against M4 bands and
    Finnish special-interest station intervals using the common overlap rule.
    Check centre frequency, occupied bandwidth, emission class, and adjacent
    channel conditions where receiver selectivity data exists.

T4. Geographic relevance
    Calculate distance and azimuth from each foreign transmitter to the Finnish
    protected station or relevant Finnish border/reference point. Record the
    coordinate source, distance formula, antenna coordinates, and assumptions.

T5. Field-strength calculation
    Use ITU-R P.1546 where applicable. Record at minimum:
      - frequency
      - path distance
      - transmitter effective radiated power or equivalent input
      - transmitting antenna height
      - receiving antenna height
      - time percentage
      - terrain/clutter/environment assumptions
      - antenna direction/directivity and polarization assumptions
      - model edition and implementation version
    If a required P.1546 input is unavailable, emit REVIEW:P1546_INPUT_MISSING.

T6. Protection criterion
    Compare calculated field strength with the applicable criterion from M4,
    CEPT/ECC material, ECC T/R 25-08, or another identified national/ITU rule.
    The exact threshold depends on the band, service, bandwidth, time
    percentage, and receiving conditions. Record the source section/table.

T7. Terrestrial error rule
    ERROR only if all are true:
      - foreign assignment is in scope;
      - frequency/channel overlap exists;
      - geographic/path conditions are satisfied;
      - P.1546 calculation is complete and reproducible; and
      - calculated field strength exceeds the cited protection criterion.

    REVIEW if the assignment overlaps but the model inputs or applicable
    protection criterion are incomplete.

Output and audit requirements
-----------------------------

Create separate result files:
  - space_3074_candidates.json
  - space_3074_errors.json
  - terrestrial_3076_candidates.json
  - terrestrial_3076_errors.json
  - interference_audit.txt

Each ERROR record must contain:
  - severity: ERROR
  - dataset and IFIC issue
  - notice and administration identifiers
  - frequency intervals and exact overlap
  - measured/calculated value
  - limit and unit
  - reference bandwidth
  - calculation/model name and parameters
  - regulatory source citation
  - reason for error
  - source database/file and source row/table
  - data and criteria version/hash

Each REVIEW record must state why it could not be classified as ERROR.
The LLM may draft correspondence only from these structured records; it must
not invent missing limits, calculations, or regulatory conclusions.

Minimum audit reason codes
--------------------------

  DATA_QUALITY
  FREQUENCY_OVERLAP
  PFD_LIMIT_EXCEEDED
  PFD_UNIT_OR_BANDWIDTH
  PFD_LIMIT_MISSING
  AP30_CRITERION_MISSING
  P1546_INPUT_MISSING
  FIELD_STRENGTH_LIMIT_EXCEEDED
  CHANGE_NEW
  CHANGE_MODIFIED
  CHANGE_WITHDRAWN
  REVIEW_REQUIRED

Final decision rule
-------------------

Do not label a case harmful interference solely because frequencies overlap.
Use ERROR only after a deterministic, cited technical criterion fails. Use
REVIEW when the data or regulatory basis is incomplete, and retain every check
and reason in interference_audit.txt.
