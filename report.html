How to read this report

  • Alerts are cases where a calculated value exceeds a cited regulatory limit: Exceeds limit (RR Table 21-4 hard limit, or T/R 25-08 trigger for terrestrial) or Coordination required (RR No. 9.14, Appendix 5 threshold).
  • Concerns are documented screening cases below alert level (near the limit, limit not applicable, worst-case inter-satellite beams, high pfd in bands without a limit). Margins are adjustable on the command line.
  • Click any row to see why it was flagged, the figures, the regulation, affected Finnish services and stations, and the recommended letter to the administration.
  • Priority orders alerts for review: ▲ High (space: ≥10 dB, ITU BR finding X/21.16, or Finnish stations in band and ≥3 dB; terrestrial: ≥6 dB or a Finnish station above the trigger), ● Medium, ○ Low (small margins, within modelling uncertainty for terrestrial <1 dB).
  • All calculations are worst case as instructed by Traficom (peak gain toward Finland; maximum e.r.p. toward the border).

Pipeline

      
    
        
  1. Ingest the BR IFIC databases (MDB converted losslessly to SQLite), the M4 frequency allocation table (Traficom open-data API) and the Traficom list of Finnish stations of special interest.
    2. 
        
  2. Scope: Space - API/A, CR/C, AP30/30A/30B and Article 11 notifications of space stations orbiting the Earth. Terrestrial - foreign land-mobile assignments (classes FB, FL, ML; NEW and MODIFIED).
    3. 
        
  3. Geometry from Finland: GSO satellites from their orbital longitude, non-GSO from perigee/apogee/inclination (every reachable elevation); terrestrial paths to the Finnish land border and territorial sea with land/sea segmentation.
    4. 
        
  4. Calculate: space pfd = e.i.r.p. in the reference bandwidth - 10log(4πd²); terrestrial field strength with ITU-R P.1546-6 (ITU-R WP 3K reference implementation).
    5. 
        
  5. Compare with the cited criterion at the actual angle of arrival / point, classify as alert or concern, one row per network or assignment.
    6. 
        
  6. Explain: reason text, regulation citation, Finnish services (M4) and stations (Excel) affected, draft letter to the administration, audit trail with input hashes.

Traficom guidance applied (SinceAI Discord)

      
    
        
  • Worst case always: satellite peak gain toward Finland; protected = RX and TX frequencies of Finnish stations, whole territory.
    • 
        
  • Any calculated exceedance is flagged; near-limit cases are concerns with documented, adjustable margins.
    • 
        
  • Terrestrial: land-mobile classes only, T/R 25-08 A1.2.3 (also above 470 MHz), P.1546-6 without the 10 dB height correction, land border and territorial sea; coordination recorded with Finland is reported, not treated as closed.
    • 
        
  • Excel: smaller of channel/transmission bandwidth; missing licence class = MUUT.

Assumptions and known limitations

    
    
      
  • Where Table 21-4 depends on constellation data not in the IFIC (functions X, Y), the less strict value is used - this can only under-report.
    • 
      
  • Inconsistent power data (integrated pwr_ds_max above pep_max) is bounded by pep_max over the emission bandwidth and labelled in the power source.
    • 
      
  • Terrestrial boundaries from Natural Earth 1:10m (public domain); territorial sea measured from the coastline, not the official baselines. Replace with the official dataset via terrestrial.py --border-file.
    • 
      
  • P.1546 without terrain profile; large lakes treated as land. Station class E5 (12 space notices) is not mapped to a service. Finnish satellite networks (ITU Space Explorer) are not in the IFIC data; the BR affected-network list for this issue names no Finnish network.
